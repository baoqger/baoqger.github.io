Company

Gapu Technology (Beijing) Co., Ltd. provides training and consulting in AI, cloud computing, and software. We are a Microsoft-certified partner in the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program (MAICPP).

Instructor

Chris Bao is a Microsoft Certified Trainer specializing in AI, Azure, GitHub Copilot, and security. He holds multiple related certifications, including AI-102, AI-103, AI-300, AZ-204, GH-300, and others.

He has delivered many public technical talks in the industry, sharing research and hands-on practice in AI, software engineering, coding agents, cloud computing, and security. See the Public Speak page for selected sessions: /public-speak/

He publishes technical articles on Medium (https://medium.com/@organicprogrammer), including pieces in Level Up Coding (343K followers) and Towards AI (144K followers). His articles focus on hands-on adoption of Microsoft AI services, AI agents, RAG, model fine-tuning, and Copilot workflows. Through hundreds of articles, he has built nearly 1,000 followers, 300 email subscribers, and thousands of clicks and reads.
